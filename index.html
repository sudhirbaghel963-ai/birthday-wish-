Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does my recipient open their keepsake? ▼

You receive a private, unlisted URL link and a printable QR code card. Simply text or email the link, or print the card. Your recipient taps the link on any smartphone or browser — no account creation, no app download, and zero ads.

Can I preview the complete film before sharing? ▼

Yes! You can preview the entire 11-scene film in real-time as you customize text, dates, photos, and secret codes. You only finalize when you’re 100% delighted.

How long does the link stay active? ▼

Your keepsake link is preserved indefinitely. Your recipient can revisit and replay their birthday celebration anytime, year after year.

What happens in the 11 scenes? ▼

The film spans 11 cinematic moments: Cupid’s arrow shot into a blossom tree, unboxing a 3D gift, an interactive PIN keepsake card, velvet curtains opening, a candle countdown with microphone blowing, a Polaroid memory twine wall, vintage scrolling film reel, interactive balloon pop wish surprises, handwritten typewriter letter, grand fireworks finale, and the clinking champagne toast.
