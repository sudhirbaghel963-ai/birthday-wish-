🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Account information: your email address (via Google Sign-In or email/password).
  • Content you provide: recipient names, personal messages, photos, videos, and music you upload to customize your Gift.
  • Payment information: processed directly by Razorpay — we do not store your card details ourselves.
  • Basic usage data: (e.g. pages visited) for improving the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, store, and deliver your personalized Gift.
  • To process payments securely via Razorpay.
  • To send you account-related emails (sign-in, password reset).
  • To provide customer support if you contact us.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Razorpay: to process payments securely.
  • Supabase: our database and storage provider, which hosts your account and Gift data securely.

We do not share your uploaded photos, videos, or messages with any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Your Gift's Recipient

Content you include in a Gift (photos, messages, etc.) becomes visible to anyone who has the shareable link to that Gift. Please only share that link with your intended recipient.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and Gift data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting valvetkeepsake@gmail.com.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, secure database policies) to protect your data, but no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting valvetkeepsake@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at valvetkeepsake@gmail.com.
